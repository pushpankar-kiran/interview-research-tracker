## 1. The tools, in one line each

| Tool | What it really is | Cost |

## 4. Honest read

**Where we are behind (today):** CleverPrep and Revarta already ship most of the
*paid* feature set — company intel, tailored questions, STAR feedback, (Revarta)
voice + delivery analytics. Final Round AI is more advanced still on mocks and
real-time help. On raw features, our Iteration 1 is a subset of what CleverPrep
sells today; we only reach rough parity around Iteration 4.

**Where we are genuinely differentiated (structural, not copyable):**
1. **Fully local & private** — no competitor is; resume + audio never leave the
   machine.
2. **$0 recurring** — everyone else is $8–99/mo or $179+/session.
3. **Persistent, self-updating research tracker** — competitors generate a brief
   once; ours is designed to stay current on a rolling 6-month basis. Nobody
   productizes this (a SaaS has no incentive to run cheap recurring research for
   one user).
4. **Enforced source discipline** — "no fact without a live source URL" is a
   code-level guarantee, not a marketing line.

**Where we deliberately DON'T compete:** real-time answer-feeding during a live
interview (Final Round's "Stealth Mode", CoPrep, LiveInterview.ai). That's
effectively cheating; we coach before/after, on purpose.

**Bottom line:** As a *product*, the base features are commoditized — we'd win only
on privacy + cost + always-current tracking. As a *personal tool for your own
prep*, this gets you CleverPrep-grade value for free and private, which is exactly
the original goal.
